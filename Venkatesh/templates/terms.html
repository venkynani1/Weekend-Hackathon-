Terms of use

Last updated 10 October 2026

1. What TalentGuard is

TalentGuard is a demonstration built for a hackathon. It ranks synthetic employee records by estimated attrition risk, explains the ranking and drafts retention actions for human review.

2. What it must not be used for

  • Hiring, firing, demotion, pay cuts, performance ratings or any other adverse employment decision.
  • Any automated decision about a person. Every output is a suggestion for a qualified human reviewer.
  • Telling an employee they are "at risk" or showing them a score. The drafted invites are deliberately neutral.
  • Scoring real people without their employer's lawful basis, an employee notice and a bias review.

3. Accuracy

Scores are statistical estimates from historical, synthetic data. On that data, 44 of the 50 people ranked highest really left, but the model will be wrong about individuals. The leave probabilities and money figures rest on assumptions stated on the Insights page.
