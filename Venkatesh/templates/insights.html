Is overtime causal?

Predictive, not proven causal. Here is how we would prove it.

Overtime predicts exits strongly, but a model trained on history cannot tell us that removing overtime keeps people. Burned-out teams may both work late and leave for a third reason.

Test design: each cycle, randomly split the 50 flagged employees into two groups of 25. Group A gets the talk in week 1, group B in week 6. Compare 90-day exits. Nobody is denied help; only timing changes, and the difference is the causal effect of the intervention.

A second, free check: employees ranked 45 to 50 and 51 to 56 are almost identical in risk but only the first group gets a talk. Comparing their outcomes over several cycles estimates the effect without any extra cost.

Until that evidence exists, the save rate on this page is an assumption, and we show what happens when it is lower.
