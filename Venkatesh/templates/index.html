Method and safeguards

Built for review, not automatic employment decisions.

The selected model is compared with two alternatives using out-of-fold average precision and capacity metrics. Sensitive demographic fields, constants, and the employee identifier are excluded from training.

Risk reasons are local counterfactual contributions: each feature is replaced with the workforce median or mode and the score change is measured. Scores support ranking within this dataset and should not be interpreted as calibrated future probabilities.

The IBM dataset is synthetic and historical. Before real use, TalentGuard would require current pre-decision data, calibration, fairness testing, privacy controls, monitoring, and qualified human review.
